Meet Nneka Hall

The woman behind the mission

A Mother's Love. A Daughter's Legacy. A Mission to Transform Care.

For Nneka Hall, pregnancy and infant loss advocacy is more than a profession. Her lived experience as a mother informs her commitment to compassionate, culturally grounded, trauma-informed education and community-rooted care.

Through the Pregnancy and Infant Loss Institute, she is working to strengthen how families are supported and how professionals learn to respond with dignity, cultural humility, and understanding.

Our purpose

Care that honors every family's experience

Our mission is to transform global pregnancy and infant loss support by establishing culturally grounded, trauma-informed, community-rooted standards of care, beginning in the U.S. and expanding to regions where grief support is underdeveloped or culturally stigmatized.

A world where every family experiencing loss receives compassionate, culturally relevant, and dignified support.
